### About

I'm a software developer who cares about writing code that's clean,
reliable, and built to last. I like solving real problems more than
chasing trends — if something works well and holds up over time, that
matters more to me than how impressive it sounds on paper.

I'm steady in how I work: I plan before I build, I test before I ship,
and I'd rather deliver something solid a little later than something
rushed on time. I'm always learning, but I try to learn things that
actually make my work better, not just things that look good on a
profile.

Outside of writing code, I care about doing things properly — attention
to detail, clear communication, and taking ownership of what I build.
